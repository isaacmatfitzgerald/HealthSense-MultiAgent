Patients admitted for an overnight or extended stay are encouraged to bring comfortable clothing, toiletries, a phone charger, and any personal items needed for daily comfort, but are discouraged from bringing large amounts of cash, jewelry, or other valuables, as hospitals cannot guarantee against loss. Most hospitals provide a secure location, such as a hospital safe, to store valuables during a stay — ask your unit's nursing station about this on admission.

Most patient rooms include a bedside table, a call button for nursing staff, a television, and Wi-Fi access, usually free of charge for patients and visitors. Many hospitals also offer a cafeteria or dining service for visitors, along with vending machines and a gift shop near the main entrance. A chaplaincy or spiritual care service is often available on request, regardless of religious affiliation. Patients should ask their care team about interpreter services if needed — most hospitals provide free interpretation, including sign language, for patients who are not fluent in English.
