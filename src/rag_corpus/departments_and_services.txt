Hospitals typically organize care into departments such as Emergency Medicine, Internal Medicine, Surgery, Cardiology, Orthopedics, Obstetrics and Gynecology, Pediatrics, Oncology, Radiology/Imaging, and Laboratory Services, among others depending on the facility's size and specialty focus. Larger hospitals, especially teaching and trauma centers, often offer more specialized services such as neurosurgery, transplant programs, or advanced cardiac catheterization labs.

Diagnostic and imaging services (X-ray, CT, MRI, ultrasound) are usually available on-site, with some requiring advance scheduling and others available on a walk-in or same-day basis for urgent needs. Rehabilitation services — physical therapy, occupational therapy, and speech therapy — are commonly offered both for inpatients and as outpatient follow-up care. For questions about whether a specific department or specialty is available at a particular hospital, or to find a doctor within a given specialty, patients can ask about doctors and services directly, since availability varies by location.
