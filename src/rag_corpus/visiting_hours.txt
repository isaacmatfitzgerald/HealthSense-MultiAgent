General visiting hours are 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM daily, though individual units may have different schedules. The Intensive Care Unit (ICU) and Neonatal Intensive Care Unit (NICU) typically restrict visits to two designated visitors at a time, with hours often limited to 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM and 3:00 PM to 5:00 PM to protect patient rest and recovery. Maternity units usually allow extended hours for a patient's partner or support person, including overnight stays in many cases.

Children under 12 may be restricted from visiting certain units, such as the ICU or oncology floors, due to infection-control concerns — please check with the specific unit's nursing station. During flu season or active outbreak precautions, visiting hours and visitor counts may be temporarily reduced hospital-wide. Visitors are always asked to check in at the front desk or unit nursing station, wear a visitor badge, and follow any posted infection-control signage (masking, hand sanitizing) before entering a patient's room.
