Each hospital's main phone number can typically be used to reach the general switchboard, which can transfer callers to a specific department, patient room, or the nurses' station for a given unit. Many hospitals also list direct numbers for common needs such as scheduling, billing, medical records requests, and the emergency department on their website or in discharge paperwork.

For directions, most hospital campuses are accessible from major roads and are marked with clear signage for Emergency, Main Entrance, and Visitor Parking, since large campuses often have multiple entrances serving different departments. If arriving by public transit, ask about the nearest stop, as many hospitals are served by local bus or rail lines with a designated hospital stop. If you're unsure which entrance to use for a scheduled appointment, procedure, or visit, it's best to call ahead — staff can direct you to the correct entrance and parking area, which can save time, especially for first-time visitors or those with mobility needs.
