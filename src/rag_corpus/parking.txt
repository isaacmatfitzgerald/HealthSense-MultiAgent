Most hospital campuses offer both a visitor parking garage and surface lots near the main entrance and emergency department. Parking is typically free for the first 30 minutes to allow for drop-off, with hourly rates afterward; many hospitals cap the daily maximum rate for visitors staying most of the day. Patients arriving for scheduled procedures should ask their scheduling department about validated parking, which is often available for surgical or same-day procedure visits.

Valet parking is available at the main entrance of many larger hospital campuses, usually from early morning until early evening, for a flat fee. Accessible parking spaces are located near all main entrances, and wheelchairs are typically available at the front desk on request. Emergency department visitors can usually park briefly in a short-term lot directly adjacent to the ER entrance. For extended stays (multi-day admissions), ask the admissions or guest services desk about discounted multi-day parking passes.
