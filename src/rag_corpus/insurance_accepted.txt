Hospitals typically accept a broad range of insurance plans, including HMO, PPO, and EPO network types, though exact participation varies by facility and by specific plan — patients are encouraged to confirm in-network status directly with their insurer before a scheduled visit, since coverage details (hospital coverage, doctor coverage, emergency coverage, diagnostic coverage) can differ significantly between plans. Emergency care is provided regardless of insurance network status, consistent with federal emergency treatment requirements.

Patients without insurance, or those receiving out-of-network care, should ask the admissions or billing office about self-pay rates and available discounts. Prior authorization may be required by some insurance plans for elective procedures, imaging, or specialist referrals — it's the patient's responsibility to confirm whether their plan requires this, though scheduling staff can often help identify what's needed. Copays are generally collected at the time of service; deductibles and coinsurance are billed after the claim is processed. Insurance cards should be brought to every visit, including follow-up appointments.
